New timeline for the Continuous Performance Test, a measure of sustained attention, vigilance and response inhibition. Letters appear one at a time under one of three rules: standard responds to a rare target letter, inhibition withholds on a rare no-go letter, and AX-CPT responds only to a probe that follows its cue. Scoring covers hits, omissions, commissions, hit RT and log-linear corrected d-prime, reported overall, per block, and per AX trial type.
