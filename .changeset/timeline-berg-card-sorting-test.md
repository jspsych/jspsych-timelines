New timeline for the Berg Card Sorting Test, a Wisconsin Card Sort variant measuring cognitive flexibility, set-shifting and perseveration. Participants match a stimulus card to one of four reference cards under a rule that shifts without warning after a run of correct responses. Scoring covers categories completed, perseverative responses and errors, conceptual level responses, failure to maintain set, and RT, with perseveration judged against a perseverated-to principle seeded from the participant's own sorting so that it is defined before the first category is completed. Styles are scoped to the timeline, `createTimeline` validates its numeric options, and the timeline units are usable on their own.
