New timeline for the two-step (two-stage) Markov decision task (Daw et al., 2011), built from the literature-derived spec: Expfactory two-stage-decision defaults and 28 options (26 from the spec, plus `response_acknowledgement_ms` and `instruction_demonstration`). Two choices per trial (a first-stage option that usually, but not always, leads to one of two second-stage states, then an option there that pays a coin with a drifting probability). The reward walk is a typed `reward_schedule` (`random_walk` with `reflect` or `reverse_step` at the bounds), and the whole schedule (transitions, probabilities, rewards, sides) is drawn when the timeline is built through `jsPsych.randomization` and recorded in the data. Scoring gives the stay probabilities by previous reward and transition, the model-free and model-based indices and the second-stage RT index; model fitting is left to analysis. An interactive walkthrough teaches the keys and, by `contingency_disclosure`, the transition structure.

Breaking: `instruction_framing` is replaced by `stimulus_set`, which takes a stimulus set (data) carrying the pictures in their roles (first-stage pair, two states with their options, colours and picture, reward pictures) and the nouns of the cover story. Bundled sets are `utils.stimuli.abstractSymbols` (default) and `utils.stimuli.spaceshipPlanetAlien`; a researcher can pass their own. Rows record `stimulus_set` and the set's ids of the pictures (`stage1_item_id`, `stage2_state_id`, `stage2_item_id`; `left_item_id`, `right_item_id`, `chosen_item_id`, `state_id` on choice rows). `text.framing_words` now takes `{ words, set }`; `instruction_test_intro`, `outcome_coin` and `outcome_none` are functions, and the reward text takes the set's reward noun. The render helpers in `utils.stimuli` take a set instead of a framing name.
