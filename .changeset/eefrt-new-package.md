New package: the Effort Expenditure for Rewards Task (EEfRT; Treadway et al. 2009), built from the literature-derived spec. Every trial: a fixation, a choice between an easy and a hard task shown with the chance of winning and both rewards, a ready screen, many rapid key presses against a time limit with a filling bar, completion feedback and reward feedback. Defaults follow the original (30 presses in 7 s or 100 in 21 s, $1.00 or $1.24 to $4.30, 12 %, 50 % or 88 % chance of winning, 5 s to choose, a 20-minute session, four practice trials with the first two assigned). The test ends on `session_duration_min` or on `n_test_trials` (`task_end_rule`); the session clock adds up the durations of the screens, so data-only simulation ends too. The effort screen is a small in-package plugin (`utils.plugins.EffortPressPlugin`) that records every press time. Typed shapes for `reward_magnitude` (`levels`, `{ min, max, n_levels }` or a per-trial `uniform` draw), `reward_probability` (proportions) and `response_window_ms` (`{ choice, easy, hard }`); currency (`text.amount`) and finger instructions (`text.finger_instruction`) are translatable text. The scorable row is the effort row (`part: "response"`) and carries the offer, the choice, `choice_forced`, the press count and times, `completed` and the reward; scoring reports the proportion of hard choices overall, by probability and by reward bin, and completion rates. An interactive walkthrough (choose the easy task, choose the hard task, do the easy task) precedes practice.
