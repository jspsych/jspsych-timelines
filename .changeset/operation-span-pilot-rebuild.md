Rebuilt from the literature-derived complex-span spec (the package keeps its name; reading, symmetry and rotation span are not implemented). Breaking: all options are snake_case under vocabulary names (`setSizes` is now `set_sizes`, `trialsPerSetSize` is `trials_per_condition`, `letterDuration` is `stimulus_duration_ms`, `mathTimeout` is `response_window_ms`, `isi` is `inter_item_interval_ms`, `recallFeedbackDuration` is `feedback_duration_ms`, `showPractice`/`showInstructions` are `practice_structure: "none"` and `instruction_format: "experimenter"`), the processing deadline defaults to the anchor's calibrated rule (mean + 2.5 SD of the practice solution times, `utils.deadlines.meanPlusSd(2.5)`) instead of no limit, practice has the anchor's three parts (letters only, processing only, combined), the scorable row is now the recall (`part: "response"`, one per trial, with `recalled_items`, `recall_correct_positions`, `set_size`) and the old per-trial `math_problems` arrays are separate `part: "processing"` rows, and the scoring result is snake_case (`partial_credit_load`, `partial_credit_unit`, `absolute_score`, `processing_accuracy`, ...). The old try-it instruction trials are replaced by an interactive walkthrough built from the task's own problems and recall input. New: block orders including `random_within_block`, per-block `set_sizes` lists, two display formats, memorandum-first order, 0-2 problems per memorandum, digits and your own word pool as memoranda, click-matrix, typed and key-press recall with serial or free order and blank/skip/? omissions, a practice criterion, mouse, touch and keyboard processing responses, `practice` overrides, deadline helpers (`meanPlusSd`, `percentile`).
